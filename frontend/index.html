HazardZ — AI-Powered Mobile Urban Intelligence Platform

Turning public transport fleets into mobile road-intelligence units. Solution Blueprint for Team Chillchat.

01

Problem Statement

Urban road hazards (potholes, cracks, waterlogging, debris) are frequent, but inspection is manual, slow, and limited in coverage. Hazards remain unnoticed until citizens report them or damage becomes severe.

02

Why It Is Unique

No dedicated inspection fleet required! Vehicles already traversing the city (public buses, sanitation trucks) become a distributed edge-sensing network with zero additional fuel/labor costs.

03

Technical Approach

  • AI / CV: YOLO-style object detection + severity scoring.
  • Location: High-precision GPS coordinates + timestamps.
  • Backend: Python / FastAPI high-throughput event ingestion.
  • Dynamic Mapping: OpenStreetMap city corridors & severity zones.
  • Alerts: Municipal prioritization engine (Alert → Verify → Prioritize → Maintain).
04

Feasibility & Mitigations

  • Lighting / Weather: Adaptive confidence thresholds (Rain: +8%, Night: +5%).
  • False Detections: Multi-frame validation + authority verification workflow.
  • Connectivity: Store-and-forward edge buffer syncs upon network return.
05

Impact & Benefits

Authorities see what needs attention first — not just what was reported last. Automated road monitoring reduces manual inspection by 85% and cuts repair turnaround times.

06

Future Scope

Predictive deterioration modeling, weather forecast correlation, citizen app verification crowdsourcing, and smart-city SCADA system integration.
